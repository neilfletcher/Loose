# Loose! A Company of Archers

A quick-fire mobile strategy game of the 1415 campaign, from the siege of Harfleur to Agincourt.

**Play it:** https://neilfletcher.github.io/loose/

## How it plays

- **Battles are short.** Place your archers on a top-down field, then loose. They shoot on their own; you tap the field to call volleys and tap portraits to fire each archer's special. Each battle lasts 30 to 90 seconds and earns up to three stars.
- **Recruit at the tavern.** Five rarities, from Common levy to four Legendary named archers, with a guaranteed Legendary within 40 recruits.
- **Every man is a person.** Recruits come with names, back stories and traits, good and bad. They level up and gain new traits. They also get wounded, and if they fall in battle they can die for good.
- **Build up the camp.** Fletcher, Bowyer, Barber-surgeon, Practice butts, Tavern and Drill yard all make the company stronger. Renown raises your rank and the size of your squad.
- **Camp events.** Choices between battles, with consequences.
- **Three chapters.** Harfleur, the March to Calais and Agincourt, six levels each with a boss at the end, plus a repeatable skirmish.

## Tech

A single `index.html`: vanilla JS, canvas rendering and Web Audio. Progress saves in the browser's localStorage. No build step.
