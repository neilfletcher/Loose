Loose! Privacy notice

Loose! A Company of Archers · last updated 27 November 2026
The short version: your game is saved on your own device. Nothing about you is sent anywhere unless you sign in to sync your saves, or say yes to sharing anonymous play stats. Nothing is sold, and there are no adverts on this site.

Who runs it

Loose! is made by Neil Fletcher, an independent developer in the UK, who is responsible for the data described here. To ask a question, or to have your data deleted, email loose@trooperlooper.co.uk and include the random ID shown in Settings, under Privacy. You don't need to give your name.

What is kept, and why

Your save, on your device. The game keeps your progress, settings and a random ID in your browser's local storage, so it carries on where you left off. This stays on your device. Clearing your browser data deletes it.

Your save in your account, only if you sign in. If you choose "Sign in with Google" in Settings, your save is stored in Google Firebase against your Google account, so your devices stay in step. Firebase receives your Google account's email address and name to sign you in. Sign out at any time; ask for deletion by email.

Anonymous play stats, only if you say yes. After your first minute of play the game asks whether you'll share anonymous stats. If you say yes, it sends how far you've got, how long you've played, your game settings and which features you've used, under the random ID, to a Google Firebase database, and turns on Google Analytics, which uses cookies to count visits and a few game events. This is used only to see what works in the game and what doesn't. If you say no, none of this runs. You can change your answer in Settings, under Privacy.

Gifts. The game checks now and then whether the developer has sent your company a gift of medals, by looking up your random ID. Nothing about you is sent.

What isn't collected

No names unless you sign in, no contact details, no location, no payment details, and no advertising profile. Nothing is sold or shared for marketing.

Loose! on other game sites

Copies of Loose! hosted on game sites such as CrazyGames send nothing to the developer and have no sign-in. Those sites show their own optional adverts and run them under their own privacy policies, which they show you.

Supporters

If you tip on Ko-fi, Ko-fi handles the payment under its own privacy policy. The game records on your device that you said you'd chipped in, and, if you share anonymous stats, that count goes with them.

How long it's kept

Anonymous stats are kept while the game is being developed and deleted on request. Account saves are kept until you ask for them to be deleted.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete data about you, or object to its use. Email the address above. If you're unhappy with the answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

Loose! isn't aimed at children under 13, and the anonymous stats contain nothing that identifies anyone.
