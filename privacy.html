Loose! Privacy notice

Loose! A Company of Archers · last updated 27 November 2026 (play stats now on unless turned off)
The short version: your game is saved on your own device. Your game is saved on your own device. The game sends anonymous play stats (how far you get, never who you are) unless you turn them off in Settings, and a daily count of games opened. Google Analytics only runs if you turn it on. Nothing is sold, and there are no adverts on this site.

Who runs it

Loose! is made by Neil Fletcher, an independent developer in the UK, who is responsible for the data described here. To ask a question, or to have your data deleted, email loose@trooperlooper.co.uk and include the random ID shown in Settings, under Privacy. You don't need to give your name.

What is kept, and why

Your save, on your device. The game keeps your progress, settings and a random ID in your browser's local storage, so it carries on where you left off. This stays on your device. Clearing your browser data deletes it.

Your save in your account, only if you sign in. If you choose "Sign in with Google" in Settings, your save is stored in Google Firebase against your Google account, so your devices stay in step. Firebase receives your Google account's email address and name to sign you in. Sign out at any time; ask for deletion by email.

Anonymous play stats, unless you turn them off. The game sends how far you've got, how long you've played, your game settings and which features you've used, under a random ID, to a Google Firebase database. This is used only to see how the game is played and what to improve, and nobody else gets it. UK law allows this without asking first, as long as you're told and can opt out: turn "Share anonymous play stats" off in Settings, under Privacy, and nothing more is sent.

Google Analytics, only if you turn it on. Analytics is Google's service and uses cookies to count visits and a few game events, so it stays off unless you switch it on in Settings, under Privacy.

An anonymous head count. Each time the game is opened, it adds one to a daily total in Google Firebase, split by whether it's a new game or a returning one, and whether it's this site or itch.io. Nothing identifies you or your device: there's no ID, no cookie, and nothing is stored on your device for it. Google's servers see your connection's IP address, as with any web request; it isn't stored with the count.

Gifts. The game checks now and then whether the developer has sent your company a gift of medals, by looking up your random ID. Nothing about you is sent.

What isn't collected

No names unless you sign in, no contact details, no location, no payment details, and no advertising profile. Nothing is sold or shared for marketing.

Loose! on other game sites

Copies of Loose! hosted on game sites have no sign-in. The CrazyGames copy sends nothing to the developer. The itch.io copy sends the anonymous head count and, unless you turn them off, the anonymous play stats; it never uses Google Analytics. Those sites show their own optional adverts and run them under their own privacy policies, which they show you.

Supporters

If you tip on Ko-fi, Ko-fi handles the payment under its own privacy policy. The game records on your device that you said you'd chipped in, and, unless you've turned play stats off, that count goes with them.

How long it's kept

Anonymous stats are kept while the game is being developed and deleted on request. Account saves are kept until you ask for them to be deleted.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete data about you, or object to its use. Email the address above. If you're unhappy with the answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

Loose! isn't aimed at children under 13, and the anonymous stats contain nothing that identifies anyone.
